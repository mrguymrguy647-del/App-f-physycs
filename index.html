How does projectile motion work?

Split the launch velocity into horizontal and vertical parts: vₓ = v·cos θ and v_y = v·sin θ.

Horizontally nothing pushes the ball (no air resistance), so x = vₓ·t. Vertically gravity pulls it down: y = v_y·t − ½·g·t².

It lands when y = 0 again, at t = 2·v·sin θ / g. Put that into the x equation and you get the range: R = v²·sin(2θ) / g. The biggest range comes from 45°, and angles like 30° and 60° give the same range!
